✨ Legacy Shop

📜 How to Play

1. Tap one of your lands, then tap a glowing neighbour to attack it.
2. With your land selected, tap Recruit to buy warriors with gold.
3. End Year to collect gold, grow armies and let rivals move.
4. Diplomacy: gifts, alliances and marriages. Allies won't attack you.
5. Dynasty: rulers age and die. Marry to have heirs. No heir means game over.
6. Rome gets stronger every year. Survive, expand and score high. Hold every land to win.
